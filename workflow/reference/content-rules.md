# Content Rules

- Preserve the article's core argument.
- Remove filler, throat-clearing, and repeated points.
- Make the video understandable without reading the article.
- Prefer one clear idea per section.
- Use concrete examples from the article when possible.
- Avoid unsupported hype.
- If the article is technical, define terms in plain language.
- If the article is long, prioritize the most useful 3-5 ideas.
- If the article is weak or vague, state the strongest defensible interpretation.

## Structure

Use 4 sections for short articles and 5 sections for longer or more complex articles.

Typical flow:

1. Hook: why this matters now.
2. Problem: what is confusing, broken, or changing.
3. Insight: the article's main explanation.
4. Consequence: what the viewer should understand or do.
5. Optional close: memorable takeaway or call to action.
