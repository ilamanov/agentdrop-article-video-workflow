# Narration Style

The narration should sound like a sharp human explainer.

Rules:

- Short sentences.
- Active voice.
- No markdown in narration.
- No citations spoken aloud.
- No "in this article" repetition.
- Avoid generic AI phrases such as "delve", "unlock", and "game changer".
- Each section should be 1-3 sentences.
- The full script should fit the requested duration.

Approximate pacing:

- 30 seconds: 70-85 words
- 60 seconds: 140-170 words
- 90 seconds: 210-250 words
